A distant panoramic view of a rustic Chinese village rooftop during autumn harvest. The rooftop is densely but neatly covered with colorful drying produce—piles of pumpkins, rows of corn, long strings of chilies, and vibrant trays of vegetables—forming an intricate, visually rich layout. Multiple layers of trees, hills, and blue-gray mountains extend into the horizon. The atmosphere is spacious, bright, and detailed, capturing the beauty and abundance of rural life.
